Core Design Assumption: FCSA is engineered to investigate suspects when you do NOT have their physical phone in custody. All tracking, location estimation, and network profiling are calculated using network-side carrier Call Detail Records (CDR) alone.
Detailed Forensic Guides: OSINT & Threat Intel AI & Predictive Analytics

1. System User Roles

FCSA segregates system administration from active investigations to maintain audit compliance and casework integrity.

User Role Primary Responsibilities
Admin (System Administrator) • Carrier Normalizer: Configures operator templates so Jio, Airtel, Vi, and BSNL CDR formats map automatically.
• AI Engine Trainer: Calibrates and trains the ML coordinate prediction weights for suspect location forecasting.
• Security Auditor: Inspects system-wide tamper-proof access logs to audit analyst casework.
Analyst (Cyber Cell Investigator) • Log Ingester: Uploads cell tower logs (CDR) into cases.
• Spatiotemporal Analyzer: Traces target tower-hopping trails, active hours, and hotspots on interactive maps.
• OSINT Investigator: Runs automated queries for WhatsApp, Signal, and historical threat databases.
• Case Exporter: Drafts legal orders and generates unified case dossier summaries.

2. Core Features of the Analyst Panel

  • The Normalization Engine: Automatically aligns varying carrier CSV formats. If one telecom sheet writes A-Party and another writes Target No, FCSA standardizes them into a single column structure instantly.
  • Geo-Intelligence Workspace: Plots suspect location histories on dynamic maps based on tower CGI coordinates. It calculates day vs. night connection ratios to help pinpoint the suspect's residential base (overnight logs) vs. operational hideouts (daytime logs).
  • Network Visualizer: Renders a visual connection web mapping the suspect to called numbers, bank logs, and towers. It flags Primary Nodes (common contacts linking multiple suspect targets) to identify syndicate heads.
  • OSINT Automation: Queries public registers and gateways in real-time to check if the target has active profiles on WhatsApp (Personal or Business) and Signal, or if they appear in historical MISP fraud databases.

3. Advanced Forensic Checks (The Smart Heuristics)

FCSA runs complex background checks on uploaded CDR logs to catch typical criminal signatures:

  • Estimated CGI Trajectories: Reconstructs chronological movement paths from tower lat/long details, mapping how the active phone line traveled from one cell tower sector to another.
  • Machine Learning Location Forecasting: Analyzes historical sequences of tower hops to predict the suspect's location drift and estimate the next tower sector they are likely to register on.
  • Impossible Velocity Warnings (SIM Cloning): Flags a critical alarm if a suspect number connects to towers separated by massive physical distances (e.g., Delhi and Mumbai) within timeframe limits that are physically impossible to travel, proving SIM cloning or multi-handset routing.
  • Financial Mule Identification: Flags SIMs receiving SMS alerts from 5 or more distinct financial institutions, or registering high-frequency bursts of outgoing SMS to UPI registration codes (like GPay or PhonePe activation gateways), indicating structured money-laundering.

4. Legal Notice & Dossier Generator

  • Section 91 CrPC Notice Drafts: Automatically generates legal notices based on case infractions. It provides templates to freeze suspect accounts at target banks, submit suspect IMEIs to the CEIR hardware block list, and request UPI logs from the NPCI.
  • Unified PDF Exporter: Compiles all maps, connection diagrams, anomalies, and notices into an official, courtroom-ready PDF dossier with a single click.
