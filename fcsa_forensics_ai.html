AI & Predictive Analytics Forensics

To track organized crime cells efficiently, FCSA integrates advanced **Artificial Intelligence (AI)** modules and **Heuristic Checking Rules**. These systems analyze raw carrier records (CDR) to estimate suspect positions, forecast location paths, and automatically flag high-risk fraud signatures.

1. Spatial Location Estimation

Because the suspect’s phone handset is not in custody, the system leverages cell tower telemetry from the CDR:

A. CGI Trajectory Replay

FCSA reads the tower IDs (First CGI) and coordinates (First CGI Lat/Long) of every event in the CDR. By chaining these coordinates chronologically, the system reconstructs the suspect’s exact movement path, showing which towers they hopped between.

B. Machine Learning Coordinates Prediction

In the Admin Dashboard, the administrator trains a **Recurrent AI Predictor** on historical tower-hopping sequences. By learning the suspect's movement patterns, the model predicts their **Location Drift** and projects their next estimated coordinate grid sector. This allows field teams to deploy assets proactively.

2. AI Heuristic Indicators (Red Flags)

FCSA’s heuristic rules run instantly upon CDR upload, evaluating data against 7 key investigative patterns:

A. Impossible Velocity (SIM Cloning Alert)

Calculates the travel speed between consecutive tower connections. If a suspect's SIM registers in Tower A (Delhi) and then Tower B (Mumbai) within a timeframe indicating a speed of **>1000 km/h**, FCSA flags a **SIM Cloning Alert** (proving SIM box operations or cloned card activity).

B. Money Mule Account Signature

Automatically detects when a single SIM registers incoming SMS alerts from **5 or more distinct financial entities** (e.g. HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Axis). A prepaid SIM acting as an alert aggregator is a classic money-laundering mule signal.

C. UPI Gateway Registration Bursts

Detects rapid, successive outgoing SMS events to UPI shortcodes (like GPAY/PhonePe gateway codes). This indicates a criminal setup binding multiple bank accounts to a newly swapped device.

D. Voice Silence Fraction

Flags numbers where over **80% of activity consists of incoming text alerts** (OTPs) and voice calls have dropped to zero. This is a telltale sign of an operational "burner SIM" bought purely to receive transaction verification codes.

3. Day vs. Night Spatiotemporal Clustering

By grouping connection timestamps, FCSA segments the suspect's habits:

  • Residential Footprint: Tower hops registered during overnight hours (10:00 PM to 6:00 AM) are clustered to identify where the suspect sleeps.
  • Operational Footprint: Daytime connections are mapped to identify their hideouts or fraud centers (e.g. local call centers or cyber hubs).
